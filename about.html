about/

90mil Radio is a broadcast and network ecosystem for artists, makers, authors and creatives exploring unconventional modes of expression, discourse and interdisciplinary practice.

Our programming operates as a node for under-represented, experimental and emergent forms of sound, linguistic and time based media arts while weaving community by offering both real and decentralised s pace for experimentation, exchange, and freedom of expression to our contributors and wider audience.

We aim to facilitate new forms of cross-pollination both within and beyond the arts.
